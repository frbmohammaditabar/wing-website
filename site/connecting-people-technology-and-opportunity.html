Who we are

Connecting People, Technology and Opportunity

← Back to Who we are

WinG Group is a Germany-based international company operating at the intersection of global talent, engineering and technology.

We help organizations strengthen their capabilities by connecting them with qualified professionals and delivering technology solutions for complex business challenges.

Our business is built around two complementary areas: Global Staffing & Recruitment and IT Consulting & Technology Solutions.

Through our international network, we identify and connect qualified professionals across Engineering, IT and Healthcare with organizations looking for specialized expertise and long-term talent.

At the same time, our international technology team provides IT consulting, project execution and technical services across Cloud, DevOps, Kubernetes, Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Automation and IT Operations.

One Vision. Two Capabilities.

What connects everything we do is simple:

People create progress. Technology enables it.

We believe organizations achieve their greatest potential when the right expertise, technology and execution come together.

That is why WinG goes beyond simply filling positions or delivering individual technical services. We aim to understand the challenge behind the requirement, identify the right approach and create solutions that deliver lasting value.

International by Nature. German by Foundation.

Based in Germany and connected to international talent and technology networks, WinG brings together global perspectives with professional German standards.

We work across borders to help companies access expertise, build stronger teams and move technology initiatives forward.

Our goal is not to be just another recruitment provider or IT service company.

We aim to become a trusted partner for organizations that want to build, transform and grow.
