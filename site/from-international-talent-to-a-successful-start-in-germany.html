Beyond Recruitment

From International Talent to a Successful Start in Germany

← Back to Recruit Top Talent

Hiring an international professional is only the beginning.

For both companies and candidates, a successful international placement also depends on what happens before and after the first working day.

That is why WinG can support international professionals with key aspects of their relocation and initial settlement in Germany.

Relocation Support

We help candidates organize and coordinate the practical steps involved in moving to Germany and preparing for their new professional life.

Accommodation Support

Finding suitable accommodation can be one of the biggest challenges for international professionals.

Where required, we support candidates with accommodation orientation, housing search guidance and practical preparation for their move.

Administrative Orientation

We help candidates understand the important practical steps involved in establishing themselves in Germany and guide them toward the relevant authorities and services.

Arrival & Initial Settlement

From preparing for arrival to understanding everyday requirements, we provide practical orientation to help candidates become comfortable in their new environment.

Professional Transition

Our goal is to reduce unnecessary friction between accepting the position and becoming fully focused on the new role.

A smoother relocation means less uncertainty for the candidate — and a faster, more focused start for the employer.

Recruitment gets the right person through the door.

A successful relocation helps them start strong.
