More Than an Ausbildung. A Career Start.

← Back to Looking for a Vocational Training

Choosing an Ausbildung is more than finding a training position.

It is a decision about your profession, your future and your life in Germany.

At WinG, we look beyond your application. We take the time to understand your education, interests, strengths, language skills and career ambitions — and help you identify Ausbildung opportunities that fit your profile.

Find the Right Career Path

Not sure which Ausbildung is right for you?

We help you understand different career paths and identify opportunities that match your interests, abilities and long-term goals.

Access Suitable Opportunities

Through our network of companies and international partners, we connect candidates with suitable Ausbildung opportunities across Germany.

International Candidate Support

Applying from outside Germany can be challenging. We provide structured guidance throughout the application process and help you understand the important steps from your first application to your future career in Germany.

Application & Interview Preparation

A strong application makes a difference.

We help you prepare your CV, application documents and interviews so you can present your motivation, skills and potential with confidence.

Language & Professional Readiness

German language skills are an important part of many Ausbildung programs.

We help you understand the language and qualification requirements for your chosen career path and prepare for the expectations of German employers.

Professional & Personal Guidance

Moving to Germany is a major decision.

Our team provides professional guidance throughout your journey, helping you understand the process, prepare properly and make informed decisions about your future.

Transparent & Confidential

Your personal information, documents and career plans are handled with the highest level of confidentiality, professionalism and care.

Let's talk about your goals

Our team will review your needs and come back with the right approach.
