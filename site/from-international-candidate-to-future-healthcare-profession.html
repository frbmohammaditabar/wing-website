Our Recruitment Process

From International Candidate to Future Healthcare Professional

← Back to Hire Apprentices & Trainees

01 — Understand Your Requirements

We learn about your organization, Ausbildung program, location, capacity and candidate requirements.

02 — Source International Candidates

We identify motivated candidates interested in starting a healthcare Ausbildung in Germany.

03 — Screen & Qualify

We assess profiles based on relevant education, motivation, language skills, professional interests and general suitability.

04 — Present Suitable Candidates

We provide your organization with carefully selected candidate profiles.

05 — Interview & Selection

We coordinate communication between your organization and candidates throughout the interview and selection process.

06 — Visa & Relocation Support

Following successful selection, we support candidates with the organizational steps associated with the visa process and relocation to Germany.

07 — Arrival & Initial Settlement

We help candidates prepare for their arrival and initial settlement so they can focus on starting their new professional journey.

08 — Ausbildung Starts

The candidate begins their Ausbildung and takes the first step toward becoming a qualified healthcare professional in Germany.
