Connecting Companies with the Right Professionals

At WinG Group, we help companies identify, attract and recruit highly qualified Engineering and IT professionals for permanent positions across Germany and international markets.

We combine technical understanding, targeted search and international talent networks to connect organizations with professionals whose expertise, experience and ambitions align with their requirements.

For international hires, our support goes beyond recruitment. We help candidates navigate the relocation process, accommodation search, initial settlement and practical transition to Germany — helping them arrive prepared, settle smoothly and focus on their new role as quickly as possible.

Whether you are looking for a highly specialized engineer, an experienced IT professional or building an entire technical team — WinG helps you find the right talent and supports the journey from recruitment to successful relocation.
